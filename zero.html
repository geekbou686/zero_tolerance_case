Declaration of Standards Against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

Our organization maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). We are committed to protecting children and ensuring that our platform is safe, secure, and free from harmful content.

Our Commitments

  • Prohibit all CSAE content, activity, and behavior on our app.
  • Implement proactive detection and moderation measures to prevent CSAE material.
  • Provide clear reporting mechanisms for users to flag suspected CSAE.
  • Cooperate fully with law enforcement and child protection agencies.
  • Regularly review and update safety policies to align with international standards.

Reporting

If you encounter any content or activity that may involve CSAE, please report it immediately through our in‑app reporting tools or contact us at: contact@innovativedealing.com.
